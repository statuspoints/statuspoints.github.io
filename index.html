                Update from a screenshot

                1. Open your airline, hotel, or card app or website to the page that shows your points and status.
                2. Take a screenshot. Got several accounts? Screenshot them all.
                3. Choose them here (one or many). Watch each one scan, then collect your points and status.

                Or drop or paste a screenshot anywhere on this page.

                Works with 50+ programs, including Delta, United, American, Marriott, Hilton, IHG, Hyatt, Amex, and Chase. See all
                Or paste text instead

                Your screenshot never leaves this device — it’s read right here in your browser. The first time, the text reader (about 6 MB) downloads from jsDelivr, so you’ll need a connection.
