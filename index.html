            Send to another device

            Open this link on your phone, tablet, or computer to copy your tracker there. Nothing is uploaded: everything travels inside the link itself.

            The link holds your whole tracker, member numbers included, so send it only to yourself or someone you trust. Opening it asks before changing anything.
